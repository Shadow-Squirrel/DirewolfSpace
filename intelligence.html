Intelligence Products

Finished intelligence on foreign space programs, capabilities, and activity.

Direwolf produces assessments, profiles, briefings, recurring reporting, and customer-specific monitoring for organizations that need to understand the space domain without standing up an intelligence organization of their own.

Why a product, not a feed

Your question does not fit a data subscription.

Data services deliver objects, orbits, and events. They do not answer the questions a decision-maker actually has.

  • What is this program capable of?
  • Why did this spacecraft do that?
  • What should we be watching?

Those answers require research, context, and a written judgment. That is what Direwolf produces.

About the platform

Process

How an engagement works

The process is deliberately simple. The requirement defines the product, and the product is written for the decision it supports.

  1. Inquiry

    Send the question or requirement to contact@direwolfspace.com or through the contact form. A general description is enough to start.

  2. Scope and handling

    Direwolf scopes the product with you: the question, the depth required, the delivery format, the cadence for recurring work, and the handling level.

  3. Research and assessment

    Direwolf researches and assesses the requirement and writes the product for the decision it supports.

  4. Delivery

    A written product, a briefing, or both, in the form agreed at scoping.

  5. Follow-on

    Additional RFIs, an executive briefing, or standing monitoring against the requirements the engagement surfaced.
