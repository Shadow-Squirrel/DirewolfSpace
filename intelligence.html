Intelligence Products

Finished intelligence on foreign space programs, capabilities, and activity.

Direwolf produces assessments, profiles, briefings, and monitoring for organizations that need to understand the space domain without standing up an intelligence organization of their own.

Why a product, not a feed

Your question does not fit a data subscription.

Data services deliver objects, orbits, and events. They do not answer the questions a decision-maker actually has.

  • What is this program capable of?
  • Why did this spacecraft do that?
  • What should we be watching?

Those answers require research, context, and a written judgment. That is what Direwolf produces.

Process

How an engagement works

The process is deliberately simple. The requirement defines the product, and the product is written for the decision it supports.

  1. Inquiry

    Send the question or requirement to contact@direwolfspace.com. A general description is enough to start.

  2. Scope and handling

    Direwolf confirms the question, the depth required, the delivery format, and the handling level. Early work is conducted in unclassified and appropriately protected environments, including CUI environments when required.

  3. Research and assessment

    Direwolf researches the requirement and assesses it, drawing on orbital and technical data, open sources, and analyst judgment.

  4. Delivery

    A written product, a briefing, or both, in the form agreed at scoping.

  5. Follow-on

    Additional RFIs, an executive briefing, or standing monitoring against the requirements the engagement surfaced.

What to expect

Handling and classification

Direwolf is designed around unclassified and appropriately protected information, including CUI environments when required. The company does not hold a facility clearance and does not accept classified work today. Supporting classified work is a future milestone that requires sponsorship for a Facility Clearance.

Do not send classified information through any channel on this site.
