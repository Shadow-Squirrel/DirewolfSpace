Submissions are delivered to contact@direwolfspace.com. Nothing is stored on this website. If you prefer, write to that address directly.

Keep the inquiry unclassified and do not include controlled unclassified information in it. If a requirement involves CUI, say so, and Direwolf confirms handling before any material is exchanged. Form delivery is handled by FormSubmit, which forwards your message to our inbox.

A useful first message

What to include

A complete first message shortens the scoping conversation. The following is sufficient for Direwolf to propose a product, format, and timeline.

  1. The question

    The question or requirement in your own words, and its scope: the countries, programs, spacecraft, regions, or technologies of interest, and the timeframe you are working to.

  2. The decision it supports

    Your organization and your role in it, who reads the product, and the decision it supports. This shapes depth and format.

  3. Format and handling

    Written assessment, briefing, recurring report, watchlist, course, or exercise. For training or exercises, the size of the group and whether virtual or on-site delivery is preferred. State whether the requirement involves CUI, and whether the engagement is direct commercial, prime, or subcontract.
