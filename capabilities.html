Continuous activity analysis

Space Domain Awareness

Space Domain Awareness grows out of the intelligence work into continuous analysis of what is happening on orbit: spacecraft behavior, orbital activity, launches, constellations, unusual activity, foreign spacecraft, watchlists, and other relevant space-domain developments.

Direwolf is not trying to be another raw satellite-tracking company. The value is the intelligence layer on top of the orbital and technical data. Instead of merely reporting that a satellite moved, the product answers the questions that follow.

Raw tracking

Satellite X moved.

Direwolf

Satellite X maneuvered, here's why it matters, which foreign program it belongs to, what similar spacecraft have done previously, what capabilities may be involved, and what we recommend watching next.

Activity on orbit

  • Launches and constellations
  • Unusual activity and foreign spacecraft
  • Customer watchlists
  • Relevant space-domain developments

Intelligence and analysis

Space Security

Your mission depends on GPS, ground systems, and RF links you do not control. Space Security addresses the threats to those systems: the spacecraft, the ground segment, the links between them, and the positioning, navigation, and timing signals that other infrastructure depends on.

Direwolf's Space Security work is intelligence and analysis. It does not include satellite penetration testing, spacecraft security engineering, or specialized offensive or defensive spacecraft security services.

Intelligence products Space Threat Fundamentals

Areas of analysis

  • Space cyber threat intelligence Cyber threats to spacecraft, ground systems, command and control infrastructure, telemetry networks, and other mission systems.
  • Ground-system security and mission infrastructure The ground segment and the mission infrastructure a space system depends on.
  • GPS, GNSS, and PNT threats Threats to the positioning, navigation, and timing signals that missions and other infrastructure depend on.
  • RF interference, jamming, and spoofing analysis Analysis of interference against RF links and GNSS signals, including jamming and spoofing.
  • Threats to critical space infrastructure Threats to the space systems that critical infrastructure and missions rely on.

Future R&D arm

Advanced Systems

Advanced Systems is where Direwolf will transition from a company that analyzes space threats into a company that builds technologies to understand and respond to them. Nothing in this area ships today.

The direction is set by the intelligence work. Problems that recur across customers become candidates for software. Software that makes Direwolf's own analysts substantially more efficient becomes a candidate for a product.

What it could eventually include

  • AI-enabled intelligence software
  • Space analytics platforms
  • RF/PNT technology
  • Cybersecurity products
  • Sensors
  • Government-funded research and development
  • Ground systems
  • Potentially, spacecraft technology
