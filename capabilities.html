Core business

Space Intelligence

Space Intelligence is where Direwolf starts and what it does every day. A customer arrives with a question about a foreign space program, a spacecraft, or a technology. A typical one: what are Iran's current military-space capabilities, what systems are operational, and where is the program likely heading? Direwolf researches it, assesses it, and delivers a finished intelligence product written for the decision the customer has to make. A question of that kind is answered by a Foreign Space Capability Assessment.

What it delivers

  • Foreign space capability assessments
  • Military space program analysis
  • Emerging technology assessments
  • Counterspace and threat analysis
  • Custom intelligence requests (RFIs)
  • Executive briefings
  • Tailored research

How an engagement runs

  1. Requirement

    The customer sends the question. Direwolf scopes the product with them by email: the question, the depth required, the delivery format, and the handling level.

  2. Research and assessment

    Direwolf researches and assesses the requirement and writes the product for the decision it supports.

  3. Delivery

    A written product, a briefing, or both, in the form agreed at scoping.

  4. Follow-on

    Additional RFIs, an executive briefing, or standing monitoring against the requirements the engagement surfaced.

Continuous activity analysis

Space Domain Awareness

Space Domain Awareness extends the intelligence work into continuous analysis of what is happening on orbit: spacecraft behavior, orbital activity, launches, constellations, unusual activity, foreign spacecraft, watchlists, and other relevant developments across the space domain.

Direwolf's value is the intelligence layer on top of the orbital and technical data. Rather than reporting that a satellite moved, the product answers the questions that follow.

Raw tracking

Intelligence, analysis, and technology

Space Security

Your mission depends on GPS, ground systems, and RF links you do not control. Space Security addresses the threats to those systems: the spacecraft, the ground segment, the links between them, and the positioning, navigation, and timing signals that other infrastructure depends on.

Direwolf delivers Space Security in two forms. The analysis tells a customer what threatens their systems and what it means for their mission. The technology, built by Advanced Systems, detects, characterizes, and geolocates RF and PNT interference and addresses cyber threats to space systems.

Intelligence products Space Threat Fundamentals

Areas of analysis

  • Space cyber threat intelligence Threat actors, tradecraft, and targeting relevant to space systems and the networks that run them.
  • Ground-system security and mission infrastructure Ground stations, mission control, and the networks and links that connect them to the spacecraft.
  • GPS, GNSS, and PNT threats Threats to the positioning, navigation, and timing signals that other infrastructure depends on.
  • RF interference, jamming, and spoofing analysis Detection, characterization, and analysis of interference against space links and PNT signals.
  • Threats to critical space infrastructure Threats to the space systems that national security and commercial operations rely on.
