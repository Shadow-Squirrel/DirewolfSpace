Foreign Space Capability Assessments

What a country assessment establishes

A Foreign Space Capability Assessment sets out a country's current military-space capabilities, the systems that are operational, and the trajectory of the program. It is the baseline against which a specific system, activity, or event is read.

  • Current military-space capabilities
  • Systems that are operational, and what they do
  • Where the program is heading

Findings are delivered to the requesting customer, not published on this page.

Public and engaged work

Where the line is drawn

The line between public and engaged work is drawn by the depth and specificity of the requirement, not by the quality of the analysis.

  1. Public

    Selected analysis, released here, that demonstrates the quality of the work so readers can evaluate it before engaging.

  2. Under engagement

    Deeper analysis, tailored intelligence, customer-specific monitoring, and standing watchlists, delivered to the customer's requirement.
