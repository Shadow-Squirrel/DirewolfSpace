Public and paid

Where the line is drawn

The line between public and paid work is drawn by the depth and specificity of the requirement, not by the quality of the work.

  1. Public

    Free pieces, published here, that demonstrate analysis quality so prospective customers can evaluate the work before engaging.

  2. Paid engagement

    Deeper analysis, tailored intelligence, customer-specific monitoring, and standing watchlists, delivered through paid engagements.
