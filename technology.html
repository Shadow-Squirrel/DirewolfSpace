Advanced Systems

The technology behind the intelligence.

Advanced Systems is Direwolf's engineering arm. It builds and operates the Direwolf Space Intelligence Analysis Platform and develops the AI-assisted analysis tooling, RF and PNT interference technology, space cybersecurity technology, and sensors and ground systems that Direwolf delivers to customers.

Direwolf Space Intelligence Analysis Platform

Not another map of dots orbiting Earth.

The Direwolf Space Intelligence Analysis Platform is the environment Direwolf analysts work in. It combines orbital data, intelligence reporting, historical spacecraft behavior, sources, watchlists, threat information and analyst notes in one place, with AI assistance throughout. An analyst selects a spacecraft and sees its orbit, operator, country, associated military program, launch information, historical maneuvers, related spacecraft, threat reporting, ground infrastructure, relevant sources and analyst assessments.

The platform underpins every Direwolf intelligence product and monitoring engagement. Customer analysts work in the same environment, with platform access scoped per engagement.

It is not a website showing dots orbiting Earth. It is the context an analyst needs to reach a judgment.

AI-assisted analysis

More information per analyst, not fewer analysts.

AI assistance in the platform does not replace the analyst. It lets one analyst process far larger volumes of space information: AI-assisted collection, correlation, anomaly detection, reporting, research and analysis, working from the same record the analyst sees. The analyst makes the call, and the analyst's judgment is the product.

Example query 01

“Summarize significant activity by Chinese military spacecraft during the past 30 days.”

Reporting: significant activity, summarized across a period, with the sourcing behind each item.

Example query 02

“Identify spacecraft whose recent behavior differs significantly from their historical behavior.”

Anomaly detection: recent behavior compared against each spacecraft's own history.

Example query 03

“What objects associated with this program have maneuvered during the past 90 days?”

Correlation: objects, programs and maneuver history, linked across the record.

Example query 04

“Generate an intelligence summary of this spacecraft and its associated foreign program.”

Research and analysis: an intelligence summary drafted for the analyst to review and finish.

RF and PNT technology

GPS is degraded in your operating area and you cannot say by what.

Direwolf builds technology that detects, characterizes, maps and geolocates RF and GNSS interference, including GPS jamming and spoofing detection and correlation, and delivers it as analysis products and as technology.

  • DetectRecognize that RF or GNSS interference is present, including GPS jamming and spoofing.
  • CharacterizeDescribe the nature and behavior of the interference.
  • MapPlace the interference in the operating area.
  • GeolocateLocate the source and correlate it with other events.

Sensors and ground systems

The systems that produce the information.

Direwolf develops sensor and ground-system technology alongside the software that analyzes what sensors collect. The analyst's requirement defines the system: what a monitoring engagement needs to observe determines what the sensor collects, and what the analyst needs to see determines what the ground segment delivers.

Sensors and ground systems are delivered as technology, and the information they produce reaches customers through Direwolf's intelligence products and monitoring engagements.
