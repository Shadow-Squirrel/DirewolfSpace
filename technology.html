Operational platform

Direwolf Space Intelligence Analysis Platform

The platform brings orbital data, intelligence reporting, historical spacecraft behavior, sources, watchlists, threat information and analyst notes into one environment, with AI assistance built in. An analyst selects a spacecraft and sees the full record behind it.

It is in daily use and underpins every Direwolf intelligence product and monitoring engagement.

Advanced development

What Direwolf is developing

Beyond the platform, Direwolf is developing state-of-the-art technology in four areas, each shaped by problems its intelligence work surfaces.

01

AI-enabled analysis

Direwolf is extending the platform's AI into machine-assisted collection, correlation, anomaly detection and reporting. The analyst still makes the call.

02

RF and PNT interference detection

Technology in active development to detect, characterize, map and geolocate RF and GNSS interference, including GPS jamming and spoofing, so an operator knows what is degrading the signal and where it originates.

03

Space cybersecurity

Direwolf is developing security technology for satellites, ground systems, command-and-control and telemetry networks.

04

Sensors and ground systems

Direwolf is developing sensing and ground-segment technology for space-domain collection.
