The company

Built to answer the questions the space domain now demands.

Direwolf Space Systems works from where every space question starts: a decision-maker who needs an answer. What can a foreign program do? What did a spacecraft just do? What does a signal in the environment mean for the mission?

We help organizations understand the rapidly evolving space domain through foreign space capability assessments, space threat intelligence, orbital and spacecraft activity analysis, RF and PNT threat analysis, and customized intelligence support.

Beyond the dot on the map

A satellite moved. The tracking feed does not say why it matters.

Tracking data reports that an object changed orbit. It does not say why the movement matters, which foreign program the object belongs to, or what should be watched next. Direwolf is not another satellite-tracking company. The differentiator is the intelligence layer on top of orbital and technical data.

Recurring intelligence

An outsourced space intelligence cell.

Direwolf serves as an outsourced space intelligence cell for organizations that depend on space but do not maintain an intelligence organization of their own. The Space Threat Intelligence Report is delivered weekly or monthly and covers the foreign military-space developments, launches, satellite activity, interference, and cyber developments a customer cares about.

Customers with more specific needs provide their own Priority Intelligence Requirements and watch requirements. Direwolf monitors against them and reports what matters.

Training and exercises

Courses for the threat. Exercises for the decision.

Three courses, Space Threat Fundamentals, Foreign Space Capabilities, and Commercial Space Awareness, give staff and leadership a working understanding of the threat environment. Tabletop exercises, from a focused two-to-four-hour session to a multi-day exercise with intelligence injects, put that understanding under time pressure.
