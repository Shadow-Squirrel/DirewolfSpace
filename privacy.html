Privacy Policy

How Direwolf Space Systems, LLC handles information you provide through this website.

Effective September 26, 2026

Who we are

This website, direwolfspace.com, is operated by Direwolf Space Systems, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("Direwolf," "we," "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@direwolfspace.com.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us. When you submit the contact form or email us, we receive:

  • Your name, organization, and email address
  • The topic you select and the content of your message
  • Any other information you include in your message

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies on this website. Our fonts and images are hosted on this site, so viewing a page does not send your information to third-party font or advertising services.

How we use it

We use the information you send to respond to your inquiry, scope and deliver requested services, evaluate supplier and teaming opportunities, and keep records of our business communications. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers

Contact form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-relay service, and are processed under that provider's own privacy terms. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and operations. Our email is hosted by our email service provider. We share information with these providers only as needed to operate the website and communicate with you.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, our customers, or the security of our systems.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond, conduct business with you, and meet legal and contractual record-keeping obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Sensitive and controlled information

Please do not send classified information, controlled unclassified information (CUI), export-controlled technical data, or other sensitive information through this website or by unsolicited email. If your requirement involves such information, tell us, and we will arrange appropriate handling before any material is exchanged.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have sent us by emailing contact@direwolfspace.com. Depending on where you live, including under Colorado law, you may have additional privacy rights, and we will honor them as required.

Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
