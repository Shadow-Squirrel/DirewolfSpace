About Direwolf

Intelligence on the space domain, and the technology that secures it.

Direwolf Space Systems is a U.S. space intelligence and defense-technology company that helps customers understand activity and threats in the space domain and builds the software, sensors and advanced systems used to understand and secure that domain.

Official wording

Mission

The mission is stated once, and the company is measured against it.

Our mission is to provide the intelligence, technology, and capabilities needed to understand emerging threats, protect critical space systems, and maintain awareness in an increasingly contested space domain. Direwolf Space Systems mission statement

The company

What Direwolf is

The public description, in the company's own words.

Direwolf Space Systems is a U.S. space intelligence and defense technology company providing advanced analysis, threat intelligence, training, and technology solutions for the national security and commercial space communities.

We help organizations understand the rapidly evolving space domain through foreign space capability assessments, space threat intelligence, orbital and spacecraft activity analysis, RF and PNT threat analysis, and customized intelligence support.

Direwolf Space Systems develops advanced software, artificial intelligence, RF/PNT, cybersecurity, and space-domain technologies that improve awareness, analysis, and decision-making in increasingly complex space environments.

Public company description

The name

Why “Space Systems”

The name is deliberate. “Space Systems” covers what Direwolf does: intelligence, software, sensors, RF and PNT, cybersecurity, ground systems, and spacecraft technology. The emphasis is on intelligence, technology, and capabilities. Direwolf produces intelligence on the space domain and builds the technology used to produce it.

Four capability areas

What Direwolf delivers

Direwolf is organized around four capability areas. Three are analytic. The fourth, Advanced Systems, is the engineering arm that builds the software, sensors, and security technology the analysts and their customers use.

01

Space Intelligence

Foreign space capability assessments, military space program analysis, counterspace and threat analysis, custom RFIs, executive briefings, recurring reporting, and training.

Intelligence products 02

Space Domain Awareness

Analysis of spacecraft behavior, orbital activity, launches, constellations, and unusual or foreign spacecraft activity, with monitoring and watchlists built around customer requirements.

Activity analysis 03

Space Security

Intelligence and analysis on space cyber threats, ground-system and mission-infrastructure security, GPS, GNSS and PNT threats, RF interference, jamming, and spoofing.

Threat analysis 04

Advanced Systems

The engineering arm. It builds and operates the Direwolf Space Intelligence Analysis Platform and develops AI-assisted analysis tooling, RF/PNT interference detection and geolocation technology, space cybersecurity technology, and sensor and ground-system technology.

The founder

Who does the analysis and the engineering

Direwolf is founder-led. The founder is a veteran with a background in military intelligence, national-security space, and cybersecurity.

The work is hands-on. The founder is directly involved in the analysis and the engineering: orbital mechanics, spacecraft behavior analysis, satellite tracking, programming, and space systems, applied directly to the company's products.
