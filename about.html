About Direwolf

Built to understand the space domain, then to secure it.

Direwolf Space Systems is being built as a U.S. space intelligence and defense-technology company that begins by helping customers understand activity and threats in the space domain and grows into developing the software, sensors and advanced systems used to understand and secure that domain.

Official wording

Mission

The mission is stated once, and the company is measured against it.

Our mission is to provide the intelligence, technology, and capabilities needed to understand emerging threats, protect critical space systems, and maintain awareness in an increasingly contested space domain. Direwolf Space Systems mission statement

The company

What Direwolf is

The public description, in the company's own words.

Direwolf Space Systems is a U.S. space intelligence and defense technology company providing advanced analysis, threat intelligence, training, and technology solutions for the national security and commercial space communities.

We help organizations understand the rapidly evolving space domain through foreign space capability assessments, space threat intelligence, orbital and spacecraft activity analysis, RF and PNT threat analysis, and customized intelligence support.

Direwolf Space Systems is building toward the development of advanced software, artificial intelligence, RF/PNT, cybersecurity, and space-domain technologies designed to improve awareness, analysis, and decision-making in increasingly complex space environments.

Public company description

The name

Why “Space Systems”

The name was chosen deliberately. “Space Intelligence” alone would describe the company today and box it in tomorrow. The emphasis is intentional: intelligence, technology, and capabilities. Direwolf is not positioned as a consulting company. It produces intelligence today and will build the technology used to produce it tomorrow.

Space Intelligence

Describes the company today. Boxes it in tomorrow.

Space Systems

Leaves room to operate, over time, in intelligence, software, sensors, RF and PNT, cybersecurity, ground systems, and potentially spacecraft technology.

Direwolf is founder-led. The founder is a veteran with a background in military intelligence, national-security space, and cybersecurity.

Where Direwolf is going

The long-range progression

The company's direction is defined as a sequence, not a promise. Each stage is expected to inform the next.

Customer problems drive the technology roadmap. Direwolf does not begin by designing a spacecraft. It begins with intelligence.

The company is at the first stage. Everything after it is written here in future tense for a reason.

Technology roadmap Advanced Systems
  1. Intelligence products Where the company is today. Founder-led assessments, briefings, custom RFIs, and training.
  2. Recurring monitoring A recurring intelligence organization: reporting and watchlists built around customer requirements, and the government contracting that follows.
  3. Proprietary space-analysis software Developed first for Direwolf analysts. If it makes them substantially more efficient, it becomes something customers can buy.
  4. AI-enabled Space Intelligence Platform Orbital data, intelligence, historical behavior, sources, watchlists, and analyst notes in one place, with AI assisting the analyst rather than replacing the analyst.
  5. RF/PNT and space cybersecurity R&D Research toward detecting and characterizing RF and GNSS interference, and toward security technology for satellites, ground systems, and mission networks.
  6. Advanced sensors and ground systems The step into larger national-security space programs.
  7. Potentially, spacecraft or spacecraft subsystem development The furthest stage on the roadmap, and the reason the company is named Space Systems.
