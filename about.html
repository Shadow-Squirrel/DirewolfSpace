About Direwolf

Intelligence on the space domain, and the technology to secure it.

Direwolf Space Systems is a U.S. space intelligence and defense technology company. It analyzes activity and threats in the space domain and develops the software and sensor technology used to secure it.

Mission

Our mission is to provide the intelligence, technology, and capabilities needed to understand emerging threats, protect critical space systems, and maintain awareness in an increasingly contested space domain.

Leadership and expertise

Direwolf is founder-led. The founder is a veteran with a background in military intelligence, national-security space, and cybersecurity, and stays hands-on in the work.

Direwolf's work is done by in-house subject matter experts in space intelligence, orbital and spacecraft behavior analysis, space security, RF and PNT, and space technology. Customers work directly with the experts doing the analysis.
